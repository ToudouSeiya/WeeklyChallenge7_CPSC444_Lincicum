Reflection Questions

1. Which material looked the most realistic?

In my opinion, the gold material (Standard) looks the most realistic. It has a range of shades from the lighting,
with highlights and shadows, and it really does look metallic. The plastic cube also looks realistic for 
the same reasons, and it also has specular highlights.

2. Which material looked the most cartoon-like?

The toon lighting, unsurprisingly, looked the most cartoon-like. It has a cel-shaded feel to it
with bands of different colors rather than a gradient of shading.

3. Which material would you use for a metal robot?

I would use the Standard material with metalicness set high, similar to the gold material.

4. How did adding the spotlight affect the scene?

While the highlights of the gold and plastic materials were hard to see before, adding the 
spotlight made it much more clear to see the unique ways that they reflect light to create
the illusion of different materials
